Sharing terms

Updated November 3, 2026.

Temporary, read-only access

Shared links let their holders view the sender’s approved trip for a limited time. Keep a link private if its route or errands are private. Only the sender can change shared progress; opening a link does not edit your own Wayday errands.

Planned route and location

The line shows the original planned route, not a guarantee of the sender’s current path or arrival time. Location may be approximate, delayed or unavailable. Shared trips are not an emergency or safety monitoring service. Phone settings, connection loss and force-stop can interrupt updates.

Google Maps

Google Maps content is subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. Keep the displayed map attribution visible.
