Sharing privacy

Updated November 3, 2026.

What you choose to share

Creating a link sends the route and fields shown in the sharing preview to Wayday’s temporary sharing service: errand titles, chosen stop locations, start and destination, and confirmed progress. Private notes and saved-place labels are excluded. Anyone holding the viewer link can read the share until it expires after two hours or the sender ends it.

Optional live location

Location sharing starts only when you choose to turn it on and allow the necessary phone permissions. While enabled, the service keeps only your latest position, its time and accuracy. Old positions are replaced rather than collected as a location history. The sender’s position is visible to link holders. One paired recipient can separately choose to share a position back; that return position is visible only to the sender. Each person can stop their own broadcast from the app or notification.

Expiry and connection loss

Expired or ended links cannot read trip data. Expired storage is removed through automatic cleanup, which may occur after access has already ended. If you stop while offline, broadcasting stops on that phone immediately and server revocation remains pending until it succeeds or the link expires. Viewers hide positions older than forty-five seconds.

Maps and hosting

The sharing viewer uses Google Maps. Loading it sends ordinary browser and map requests to Google; it does not upload your phone location unless you explicitly enable sharing in Wayday. Wayday adds no analytics or advertising and does not put trip details or link credentials in access logs or link previews.
