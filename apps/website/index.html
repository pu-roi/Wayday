  2. Open the downloaded file on your Android phone.

A few things
before you head out.

Clear answers for an app that’s still taking shape.

Can I download Wayday now?

Public testing is being prepared. The download button will become available when an Android release has passed qualification. Wayday is not currently offered through Google Play.

Can I try it in my browser?

This is Wayday’s information and download website. The application runs on Android; there is no interactive browser demo here.

Does it work without internet?

Locally saved tasks and progress remain on your phone. New interpretation, place discovery, fresh route estimates and navigation need internet. Local storage does not mean complete offline planning.

Does Wayday decide everything for me?

No. You review interpretations, confirm necessary details and can change recommended places. You mark tasks complete yourself. Estimates do not guarantee travel times, opening hours, inventory or appointment availability.

What does “recurring” mean?

Recurring lists and reusable trips help you bring familiar errands into a new day. Repeat settings do not automatically schedule errands. Reminders and live sharing are not currently available.

How do I update the app?

Download the newer official APK and approve the update on Android. Keep the existing app installed so a compatible update can preserve your local data. We do not silently install updates.

Personal plans.
Clear boundaries.

Your errands, saved lists and trip progress are stored locally in the Android app. Relevant text and locations are sent to online providers when a feature needs them; we do not claim that every piece of data always stays on your device.

Trip sharing uploads the route and fields you approve for a temporary two-hour link. Live location starts off and requires your consent. Anyone with the link can view the sender’s shared trip; a paired recipient’s optional return location is visible only to the sender. Shared trip viewers use Google Maps and show their privacy and terms.

This website has no accounts or app-provider requests and adds no analytics. GitHub Pages, which hosts it, may process visitor information under GitHub’s privacy statement. Android’s permission controls remain yours.
