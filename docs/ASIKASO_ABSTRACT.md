## **Abstract**

Completing daily errands while commuting can require users to switch between navigation applications, reminders, and business listings. This fragmented process makes it difficult to organize multiple stops, select suitable transportation options, and complete tasks within a limited amount of time. ASIKASO is a proposed smart commute and errand management application designed for students, workers, and public transportation users. The application integrates multimodal route navigation with task management to help users efficiently plan their journeys, organize daily responsibilities, and accomplish multiple errands within a single trip.

ASIKASO recommends suitable stores and service locations based on the user's planned route, accessibility, operating hours, and estimated travel time. It organizes multiple destinations according to task priorities, appointments, deadlines, and dependencies while considering the user's available time and preferred transportation options. Users can customize their travel preferences, such as minimizing transportation costs, reducing walking distances, avoiding unnecessary transfers, or selecting faster routes. Additionally, the application provides location-based reminders, errand checklists, progress tracking, and saved routines to improve task organization. When unexpected delays or changes occur, ASIKASO dynamically adjusts the remaining itinerary and recommends postponing optional errands to prioritize essential tasks.

By integrating intelligent route planning and daily errand management, ASIKASO aims to minimize unnecessary travel, improve time management, reduce transportation expenses, and provide a more convenient commuting experience. The proposed application's effectiveness will be evaluated based on route efficiency, task completion rates, estimated and actual travel times, and user satisfaction. Ultimately, ASIKASO seeks to provide a practical and accessible solution that helps commuters accomplish their daily responsibilities more efficiently while making their journeys more organized and productive.
