**Two priorities**

1. **[P1] Simplify adding and editing errands.** Today stacks Write/Pick, templates, the notepad, an interpretation list and Advanced settings. The same errands appear repeatedly. In the default mobile screenshot, this pushes To, the deadline and travel mode below the initial viewport. Expanded editors also consume almost an entire screen.

   Keep natural-language entry, but resolve it into one compact numbered list. Tap a row to edit one errand in a focused screen or sheet. Show its place and time rule concisely; reveal duration, priority and ordering when needed. This preserves interpretation transparency while reducing scrolling and competing controls. Suggested direction: Impeccable distill/shape.

2. **[P1] Make correction and recovery more trustworthy.** “Move to tomorrow” currently removes the errand without storing it for tomorrow. Fixed-time conflicts say that two times clash without identifying the affected errands. Changing a note line also recreates that errand, discarding its previous structured corrections.

   Preserve deferred errands, name the conflicting stops, and make reinterpreting an edited sentence predictable. Today’s Pick editor also exposes Flexible/Fixed/Closing, although CONTEXT.md and PRODUCT.md reserve those labels for templates; DESIGN.md currently describes the conflicting behavior. Suggested direction: Impeccable clarify/harden.
